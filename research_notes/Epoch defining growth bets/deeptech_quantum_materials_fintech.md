## Q2. Which categories have a credible 2027–2032 catalyst that could re-rate by an order of magnitude?

### Takeaway
Quantum (fault-tolerance demonstrations plus public-market comps), next-gen lithography and fabs (first wafers and a credible non-ASML path), and AI-for-materials (a first commercially validated discovery) have true 10x binary catalysts. Manufacturing and fintech have strong but more linear compounding. Climate-industrial catalysts are slower and capital-intensive, and recent recapitalizations at Stegra and Boston Metal show the downside.

### Cited Findings
- **Quantum: public comps exist.** Quantinuum IPO'd at $60/share (June 2026), raising $1.68B at ~$12.7–15B, on Q1-2026 revenue of only $5.2M. The listed pure-plays (IonQ, D-Wave, Rigetti, QCi) total ~$36B in market cap, and Xanadu is ~$5B via SPAC. This sets the exit multiples a fault-tolerance milestone could command. — [IPO Scoop](https://www.iposcoop.com/the-ipo-buzz-quantinuum-priced-upsized-ipo-at-60-5-above-range/); [Conyers](https://www.conyers.com/publications/view/quantinuum-inc-us1-68-billion-initial-public-offering/); [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/quantum-computing-startup-investment-data-quantinuum-ipo/)
- **Quantum: catalyst timing.** PsiQuantum targets operational Chicago systems around end-2027 to 2028. Oratomic is explicitly targeting fault tolerance with ~20K qubits. — [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/oratomic-raises-300m-for-development-of-20000-qubit-fault-tolerant-quantum-computer/); [Quantum Zeitgeist](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/psiquantum-quantum-computing-quantum-deployment/)
- **Lithography and fabs.** Substrate claims X-ray lithography could cut costs dramatically; one headline claims 90%, which is a company claim. xLight has US government CHIPS backing and plans to supply ASML. Fab2 aims to mass-produce compact fabs. — [ic-pcb](https://www.ic-pcb.com/substrate-bets-on-x-ray-lithography-to-challenge-asml-and-slash-chip-production-costs-by-90.html); [aiweekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/xlight-in-talks-to-raise-350m-after-us-chips-act-award); [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/149546-formerly-atomicsemi-fab2-raises-500m-series-a-at-a-3-7b-valuation-to-mas/)
- **Independent skepticism on X-ray lithography viability** — [Vik's Newsletter](https://www.viksnewsletter.com/p/an-in-depth-look-at-the-viability)
- **Climate-industrial downside evidence:** Boston Metal's Brazil accident and cash strain; Stegra's Boden mill timeline "under review." — [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/05/20/1137523/boston-metal-funding-critical-metals/); [Canary Media](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/green-steel/stegra-funding-complete-mill)
- **Antora** frames its Series C around surging power demand and reindustrialization, a demand pull that ties it to AI-datacenter power, not only to decarbonization policy. — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260730278399/en/Antora-Closes-$550-Million-in-Series-C-Funding-to-Meet-Surging-Energy-Demand-and-Reindustrialize-America)
- **Agent payments** are still seed/Series A, with $115M raised sector-wide in 2026. — [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/charts/startup-stack-ai-spending)

### Inferences
- Ranked by order-of-magnitude re-rating potential over 2027–32: (1) quantum, binary on fault tolerance; (2) lithography/fab, binary on first production wafers and customer qualification; (3) AI-for-materials, binary on a commercially validated discovery such as a room-temperature superconductor, a better battery chemistry, or sorbents; (4) AI-datacenter-linked energy and industrial heat (Antora, TAR); (5) manufacturing (Hadrian, Divergent), which is driven by defense budgets and compounding; (6) fintech, which is mature, priced on revenue multiples, with order-of-magnitude upside only in nascent agent-payment rails that are still too early for her stage.
- Defense and AI-power demand are now the dominant drivers of "reindustrialization" valuations, not climate policy.

### Gaps
- No primary source was found for specific fault-tolerance timelines for QuEra, Atom, Photonic, or Alice & Bob in 2026.
- No AI-for-materials company has a publicly verified commercial discovery yet (inferred from the critique essay; not exhaustively verified).

The only names where it is even arguable are those entering at ≤~$3B with an epoch-defining binary outcome: Oratomic, Substrate, xLight, Photonic, Machina Labs, Orbital, and Fab2. Everything priced above ~$7B (PsiQuantum, Hadrian, Periodic, Ramp, Revolut, Kraken) is a 3–10x bet, not a 50x bet.

- For a GP with a 50x bar at Series B+, the most actionable set is: (a) Spark's own Oratomic follow-on; (b) Substrate or xLight next rounds; (c) Photonic; (d) neutral-atom or cat-qubit peers (QuEra, Atom, Alice & Bob) if they price ≤$2–3B; (e) Machina Labs and Orbital if they are priced ≤$1.5B.

- Scores are judgment calls, not sourced facts. Undisclosed valuations, marked "?", make the 50x math indeterminate for roughly half the list. Primary-source confirmation is needed before acting on PsiQuantum ($10.5B), Periodic ($7.5B close), CuspAI ($2.6B), and Divergent ($3.37B).
