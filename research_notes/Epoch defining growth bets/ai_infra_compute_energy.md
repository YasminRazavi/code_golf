# AI Infrastructure, Compute & Energy-for-AI: Growth-Stage Private Candidates (as of 30 Oct 2026)

Scope: private, Series B to late stage, HQ North America or Europe. The lens is 50x from entry, so a company's "50x bar" is 50x its latest valuation. Research ran on 30 Oct 2026. Many valuation figures come from secondary aggregators (valueaddvc, newmarketpitch, wowtale). Where only those sources exist, the claim is flagged "secondary".

## Q1: Candidate companies (HQ, latest round, valuation, raised, leads, Spark involvement, traction, 50x bar, score)

### Takeaway
Private AI infra valuations re-rated sharply during 2026. Etched ($21B, with bids at $40-50B), Crusoe ($30.9B), Fluidstack (~$18B), Fireworks ($17.5B, in talks at $30-40B), Helion ($15.5B), Nscale ($14.6B) and Baseten ($13B; Spark co-led) all imply exits of $650B to $2T+ for a 50x. The more plausible 50x entries are sub-$7B names with an architectural edge or a possible regime change: MatX, Ayar Labs, Lightmatter, Proxima Fusion, Unconventional AI, Valar Atomics, Tenstorrent, Together AI and d-Matrix. Spark is confirmed as an existing investor in Baseten (co-led the Series F) and RadixArk (co-led the seed).

### Inferences
- Entry price is the binding constraint. Every company above about $10B needs a top-20 global market cap for 50x. The 50x zone is the $2-7B band of architectural bets in silicon, photonics and advanced energy.
- Spark already sits in the inference software layer (Baseten, RadixArk). The portfolio gap is physical-layer exposure: silicon, photonics and power.
- Jane Street's appearance as lead in Etched, MatX and Fluidstack suggests sophisticated trading firms are using their own inference workloads as technical diligence. That is a useful signal for MatX in particular.

## Q2: Sub-sector crowding vs. underpricing; where the AI capex cycle is heading

### Takeaway
Hyperscaler capex for 2026 is about $720-745B across the big four and is guided up "significantly" for 2027, with possibly more than $1T total. However, there were first signs of hesitation in late October, when Microsoft and Google put off raising their forecasts. Neoclouds and inference platforms look the most crowded and expensive. Photonics, sub-$5B silicon and European/advanced energy look relatively underpriced given that power and interconnect are the binding bottlenecks.

### Cited Findings
- Meta raised the low end of its 2026 capex guidance to $135B, keeping the top at $145B (Oct 2026). Alphabet's 2026 guidance is $195-205B with a "significant" increase in 2027. Amazon's guidance is about $220B. The big-four total is about $720-745B (midpoint about $732.5B) — [TMT Finance](https://www.tmtfinance.com/intel/2026-hyperscaler-capex-tops-us700bn-analysis); [404K Research brief, 30 Oct 2026](https://404kresearch.substack.com/p/456x-morning-brief-october-30-2026-cc7)
- CNBC (29 Oct 2026): "AI buildout hits snag as Google and Microsoft put off raising capex forecasts". Microsoft sees capex accelerating in 1H 2027. Hyperscaler capex is expected to top $1T next year — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/29/microsoft-google-ai-capex-estimates.html)
- One brief says Microsoft trimmed calendar-2026 capex from $175B to $170B, shifting spend into 2027. This is unconfirmed — [404K Research](https://404kresearch.substack.com/p/456x-morning-brief-october-30-2026-cc7)
- The same brief notes bottlenecks spreading and memory price hikes squeezing device margins — [404K Research](https://404kresearch.substack.com/p/456x-morning-brief-october-30-2026-cc7)
- Oracle's FY2027 capex guidance is $90-95B; another source says about $70B — [TMT Finance](https://www.tmtfinance.com/intel/2026-hyperscaler-capex-tops-us700bn-analysis)
- More than $15B changed hands in silicon photonics between late 2025 and mid-2026, much of it via M&A (Marvell–Celestial for $3.25B) — [optim.vc](https://www.optim.vc/the-state-of-silicon-photonics-whos-building-whos-buying-and-where-the-industry-is-heading/)
- Valuation step-ups during 2026 show crowding: Etched went from $5B to $21B in about 8 months, with bids at $40-50B. Fluidstack went from $7.5B to $18B in about 8 months. Valar went from $2B to $6B in about 5 months. Baseten went from $5B to $13B in 4 months — sources as in Q1.

### Inferences
- **Over-crowded:** GPU neoclouds (Crusoe, Fluidstack, Nscale and Lambda all at $14-31B, capital intensive, IPO-bound). Inference platforms (Fireworks, Baseten, Together) have fast revenue growth but face token price deflation and hyperscaler competition. Transformer-specific ASICs such as Etched are now priced for success.
- **Relatively underpriced:** Optical interconnect/CPO (Ayar, Lightmatter), where the copper scale-up limit is a hard physics bottleneck and the incumbents (Marvell, Nvidia, Broadcom) are acquirers. European energy and compute (Proxima at €2.4B against US fusion peers at $15B+). Next-paradigm compute (Unconventional, MatX).
- **Bubble risk:** capex is still rising but decelerating at the margin (Microsoft and Google holding guidance). A 2027 digestion phase would hit neocloud valuations first, because they depend on leverage and contracted offtake. Energy and photonics have longer duration and would be less affected.
- **Power is the bottleneck:** an implied $1T+ in 2027 capex needs tens of GW of new power. This supports energy assets, but fusion and SMR timelines (2028-2035) lag the capex cycle. Near-term winners are gas, geothermal and behind-the-meter providers.

### Gaps
- Amazon, Microsoft and Alphabet's Q3 2026 earnings details were not fully captured. The sources conflict on which companies had already reported.
- No SemiAnalysis or primary data on GW-level power queue numbers was gathered.
