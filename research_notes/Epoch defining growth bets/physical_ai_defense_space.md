## 2. Hype cycle for humanoids and robot foundation models: real deployment data vs demos, the data-scaling thesis, and who holds the data advantage

### Takeaway
Capital is at peak-hype levels while deployments are still pilot-scale. The best public humanoid data is Figure's BMW pilot (about 1,250 operating hours) and Agility's about 65,000 cumulative hours across 9 sites, both company-reported. Robot foundation model valuations doubled in months (PI at $11B to $20B, FieldAI 5x) with thin revenue. The exceptions are Skild and FieldAI, at more than $100M each.

### Cited Findings
- **Figure at BMW Spartanburg.** Figure 02 ran for about 11 months, handling more than 90,000 sheet-metal parts over about 1,250 operating hours. BMW called it a pilot. The 02 units were retired, and Figure 03 moved to logistics sequencing at the plant. A claim of more than 350 Figure 03 units built is company-reported — [Physical AI Journal](https://www.physicalaijournal.org/post/humanoid-deployment-evidence-report-2026); [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/07/18/humanoid-robots-in-2026-what-is-actually-deployed/)
- **Agility Digit.** More than 65,000 operating hours across 9 customer facilities: GXO, Schaeffler, Toyota Canada and Mercado Libre. More than 100,000 totes moved at GXO. These figures come from SPAC/S-4 disclosures and are not independently verified. RoboFab capacity is up to 10,000 units a year — [Physical AI Journal](https://www.physicalaijournal.org/post/humanoid-deployment-evidence-report-2026); [thetechtrends](https://thetechtrends.tech/humanoid-robots-in-warehouses/)
- **Unitree.** About 5,500 humanoids shipped in 2025, but only about 250 in real-world labor deployment as of mid-2026 — [Physical AI Journal](https://www.physicalaijournal.org/post/humanoid-deployment-evidence-report-2026)
- Figure's deployments are characterized as "relatively small numbers of robots at customer sites" — [newmarketpitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/humanoid-robotics-top-startups-fundraising)
- Physical Intelligence: no revenue, pilot-style partner testing — [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/10/san-francisco-robot-brain-startup-nears-20-billion-valuation-with-zero-revenue/). BigGo frames the round as igniting a "robot AI bubble debate" — [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/87e1c681-2ec3-4c32-ab76-27ede72df79f)
- Skild reports more than $100M ARR and FieldAI more than $100M revenue from more than 30 customers — [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/10/san-francisco-robot-brain-startup-nears-20-billion-valuation-with-zero-revenue/)
- Robotics VC reached $9.9B in Q3 2026, versus $4.3B a year earlier — [Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/10/san-francisco-robot-brain-startup-nears-20-billion-valuation-with-zero-revenue/)
- 1X has about 10,000 NEO pre-orders, which are not deliveries — [Robonomy](https://robonomy.net/news_briefs/major-players-2026-figure-ai-39b-1x-factory)

### Inferences
- **Hype cycle position.** Humanoids are near the "peak of inflated expectations." Tens of billions in valuation sit on fleets of hundreds of units and pilots measured in thousands of hours. Robot foundation models are slightly earlier, but valuations are re-rating faster than evidence. The first public signals of the trough would be a down round or a failed SPAC (watch Agility).
- **Data-scaling thesis and who holds the data advantage.** The bet is that cross-embodiment data plus scale produces generalization, as it did for LLMs. There are three data strategies:
  - (a) Teleoperation and real-world fleet data. PI and Dyna collect real robot data. Figure, Agility and 1X hold their own fleet logs. Tesla is the largest potential holder, but it is public.
  - (b) Simulation and synthetic data. Genesis AI and Skild are sim-heavy, and Waabi uses this approach in AV.
  - (c) Deployment-revenue data flywheels. FieldAI (rugged and industrial sites) and Skild (claimed ARR) fit here.
  - In my judgment, revenue-generating deployment is the most defensible data moat. In autonomy, Waymo and Tesla hold the data advantage. This is inference, not sourced.
- **Chinese competition.** Unitree's volumes compress hardware margins for Western humanoid makers.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-company) data on humanoid uptime, intervention rate or unit economics was found.
- No primary source on PI's dataset size or on its newer model releases was found in this pass.
- Skild's ARR composition (which customers, hardware or software) is unverified.

## 3. European defense rearmament: budget figures and which startups are capturing it

### Takeaway
NATO allies committed at The Hague (June 2025) to 5% of GDP by 2035: 3.5% core defense plus 1.5% security-related. They reaffirmed this at the July 2026 summit. The EU's SAFE loan instrument adds €150B. European defense startups raised at sharply rising valuations in 2026: Helsing $18B, Quantum Systems $8B, Tekever $6.4B, Stark €3.5B+, Cambridge Aerospace $3.4B. Drones and air defense are the capture categories.

### Cited Findings
- The 5%-by-2035 commitment is 3.5% core defense plus up to 1.5% for broader security spending — [NATO](https://www.nato.int/en/what-we-do/introduction-to-nato/defence-expenditures-and-natos-5-commitment); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agreement_on_5%25_NATO_defence_spending_by_2035)
