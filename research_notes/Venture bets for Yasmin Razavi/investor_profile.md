## Q2. What is Kindred Ventures: funds, vintages, stage, check size, ownership, founders, approach?

### Takeaway
Kindred is a San Francisco early-stage generalist firm with a frontier-tech focus, run by managing directors Steve Jang and Kanyi Maqubela. Funds: $56M (2019), $101M (2021), $200M Fund III plus a $112M Selector I opportunity fund (2022, $312M total), and a $355M Fund IV plus Selector II set announced June 8, 2026. Typical checks are roughly $250K to $3M at pre-seed and seed, though it now also leads $10M to $100M rounds.
